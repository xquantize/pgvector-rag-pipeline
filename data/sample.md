## Acme Widget Spec

The Acme Widget Model X was released in March 2024. It weighs 250 grams and
runs on a rechargeable lithium-ion battery rated for 12 hours of continuous use.

The warranty covers manufacturing defects for two years from the purchase date.
Water damage and intentional misuse are not covered.

Support contact: support@acme-widgets.example. Phone support is available
Monday through Friday, 9:00–17:00 Pacific Time.
